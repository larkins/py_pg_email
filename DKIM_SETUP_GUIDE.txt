STEP 2: SPF Record (CRITICAL - prevents spoofing)
================================================================================

Add this TXT record to your DNS settings:

Host/Name: protophysics.com.au (or @ for root)
Type: TXT
TTL: 3600 (or default)
Value:
v=spf1 ip4:144.6.112.4 -all

Explanation:
- ip4:144.6.112.4 = Your server's IP address
- -all = Fail all other IPs (strict policy)

Alternative (if you send from multiple IPs):
v=spf1 ip4:144.6.112.4 include:_spf.google.com ~all

================================================================================
STEP 3: Reverse DNS (PTR) Record (CRITICAL)
================================================================================

Contact your ISP or hosting provider to set up a PTR record:

IP: 144.6.112.4
Should point to: protophysics.com.au

This is usually configured by your ISP, not in your DNS panel.

================================================================================
STEP 4: DMARC Record (Recommended)
================================================================================

Add this TXT record to your DNS settings:

Host/Name: _dmarc.protophysics.com.au
Type: TXT
TTL: 3600 (or default)
Value:
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@protophysics.com.au; pct=100

Explanation:
- p=quarantine = Failed emails go to spam (not rejected)
- rua = Where to send reports
- pct=100 = Apply to 100% of emails

   - All should show "PASS"

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: Emails still in spam
Solution: 
- Wait 24 hours for DNS propagation
- Check https://www.mail-tester.com
- Verify reverse DNS is set up

Problem: DKIM verification fails
Solution:
- Check the DNS record value matches exactly
- Ensure no extra spaces or line breaks
- Use online DKIM validator

Problem: SPF verification fails
Solution:
- Verify your public IP is correct (144.6.112.4)
- Check with: curl ifconfig.me

Problem: Gmail rejects emails
Solution:
- Ensure DKIM, SPF, and reverse DNS are all correct
- Check Gmail Postmaster Tools: https://postmaster.google.com
- Look for IP reputation issues
